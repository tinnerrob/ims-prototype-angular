What each one does
Load sample data
Parties and suppliers, price cards, locations and their types, tax schedules, overheads, the catalog with its shelves and movement ledger, orders, purchase orders and their receipts, inspections, work orders, timesheets, sub-rentals, the fleet, dispatches, invoices, documents, count sheets, and the business types with their categories and form schemas. The rows are the fixture's own — same ids, same totals — so every page reads exactly as it does on a first load.
Remove all sample data
Deletes all of that. What stays is the workspace itself: the tenant, its people, the session, the yard, and the settings that say how it behaves — pricing, the receiving rules and the stock set's shape. The business type is cleared with the row it named, so the workspace simply has none until you give it one.

is the narrower one, on Business type & Categories: it drops the catalog and the records that name it but keeps your partners, locations and settings — the move for “keep my workspace, replace its catalog”.
